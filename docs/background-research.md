# Background research: Why is the Israeli startup ecosystem so effective?

*TAMID Fall 2026 Israel Connection Creative Project, Group 3. This page is the foundation for our game, **Next Stop: Tel Aviv**. Each of the game's six questions leads into one of the facts below.*

**Short answer.** No single cause explains it. Five forces reinforce each other: dense talent networks, a culture of learning from failure, public money that pulled in private money, research with a path to market, and a small home market that pushes companies to think globally. Israeli founders come from many different backgrounds, so treat these as tendencies, not rules.

### 1. Connected talent and networks
In a small country, founders, engineers, investors and researchers often know one another through universities, workplaces and national service. Wix co-founder Avishai Abrahami calls "paying it forward" the secret ingredient. When Wix was young, other founders gave intros and advice because "we were helped when we were starting out." *(This is one founder's view, not a measured fact.)* Networks also reach users: Waze built its map from a community of drivers sharing live traffic. When Google acquired Waze in 2013, it said the product team would stay in Israel.
Sources: [Entrepreneur, Abrahami 2013](https://www.entrepreneur.com/article/228159) · [Google blog, 2013](https://blog.google/products/maps/google-maps-and-waze-outsmarting/)

### 2. Learning and adaptation
Waze co-founder Uri Levine talks openly about failed ventures as part of the job, and argues that founders should "fall in love with the problem, not the solution": keep the problem fixed and change the fix. Attitudes toward failure vary from person to person, but well-known founders model it publicly.
Sources: [Tel Aviv University, The Waze Forward](https://english.tau.ac.il/node/2156) · [VC Cafe on Levine's book](https://www.vccafe.com/uri-levines-new-book-is-a-reminder-for-founders-to-put-the-problem-first/)

### 3. Government support plus private investment
- **Yozma (1993).** About $100M of public money co-invested in ten new venture funds. Each fund paired local managers with foreign VC partners, and private partners could buy the state's share later; most did. Analysts see Yozma as one catalyst among several, not the whole story.
- **Technological incubators (1991).** Started during a large wave of immigration from the former Soviet Union to help scientists turn inventions into companies.
- **R&D intensity.** About 6.3% of GDP in 2023, the highest in the OECD, and mostly business-funded.

Sources: [OECD, Government support for VC: Israel](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/benchmarking-government-support-for-venture-capital_82cd3fe1-en/israel_b5c8cc2e-en.html) · [GEN, Yozma](https://www.genglobal.org/atlas/yozma) · [Jewish Virtual Library, Incubators](https://www.jewishvirtuallibrary.org/technological-incubators-in-israel) · [SSTI on OECD R&D data](https://ssti.org/index%2ephp/node/86669)

### 4. Research that becomes products
Universities run technology-transfer companies. The Hebrew University's **Yissum** (founded 1964) licensed the computer-vision research by Prof. Amnon Shashua that led to **Mobileye** (founded 1999). Intel acquired Mobileye in 2017. **Netafim** began in 1965 at Kibbutz Hatzerim in the Negev, turning Simcha Blass's drip-irrigation invention into a global company. A local constraint, scarce water, became an export.
Sources: [ISRAEL21c, Yissum at 50](https://archive.israel21c.org/50-years-of-bringing-brilliant-ideas-from-lab-to-market/) · [Mobileye, About](https://www.mobileye.com/about/) · [Negev Foundation, Simcha Blass](https://www.negev.org/simcha-blass-drip-irrigation/) · [Times of Israel, drip irrigation](https://www.timesofisrael.com/what-israeli-drips-did-for-the-world/)

### 5. Thinking globally from day one
Israel has about 10.2 million residents (CBS estimate, end of 2025), so most startups must design for customers abroad early. High-tech made up about 56% of Israel's exports in 2024, according to the Israel Innovation Authority.
Sources: [JNS on CBS data](https://www.jns.org/israels-population-hits-10178000/) · [Israel Innovation Authority, 2025 report](https://innovationisrael.org.il/en/report/the-israeli-high-tech-sector/)
