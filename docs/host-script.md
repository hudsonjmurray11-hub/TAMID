# Host script: Next Stop: Tel Aviv (≈ 4:00)

**Before class:** `/host` is on the projector (fullscreen), sound is optional, and **4 min (default)** is selected. `/demo` is open in a second tab showing **Offline ready**.

**Speakers.** Each teammate owns one part, so all four are visible:
- **N** = Narration & presentation (Sienna): runs the clicker / Space bar.
- **R** = Research & content (Charlie): reads the roadside facts.
- **G** = Game logic (Hudson): explains the rules.
- **V** = Visual design (Carter): calls out the journey.

Lines in *italics* are optional if you're ahead of time. Answer timers advance on their own, so speak while people type.

---

### 0:00–0:20 · Boarding (lobby, on screen: QR + code)
**N:** "Phones out! Scan the code or go to the address on screen. You're all riding in our van to Tel Aviv."
**G:** "Six easy prompts. Type one answer each. The rarer your answer, the further you go: common is ten kilometres, a hidden gem is fifty."
→ When most names are in, **N presses Space** (*Start the trip*).

### 0:20–0:55 · Stop 1: *Something that uses a lot of water*
*(25 s answering)* **V:** "Watch the van. Every answer moves the leader's van down the coast, and Tel Aviv gets closer on the horizon."
*(10 s reveal)* **R:** "Much of Israel is desert, so water is precious. Netafim started on a desert kibbutz in 1965 with drip irrigation, and now farms worldwide use it."

### 0:55–1:30 · Stop 2: *Something that makes a road trip annoying*
*(answering)* **G:** "Typo? Unrecognised answer? Just try again before time's up."
*(reveal)* **R:** "Stuck in traffic? Waze, built in Israel, fixed that by letting drivers share traffic with each other. Google bought it in 2013 and kept the team in Israel."

### 1:30–2:05 · Stop 3: *A way to raise money for a project*
*(answering)* **G:** "Asking your parents is ten kilometres. Think bigger."
*(reveal)* **R:** "In the early '90s Israel had very little venture capital. In 1993 its Yozma program used public money to pull private and foreign investors into ten new venture funds."

### 2:05–2:40 · Stop 4: *Something a self-driving car needs to notice*
*(answering)* **N (Pursuing Innovation):** "This is **Pursuing Innovation**: stop signs are obvious. What would *you* notice that an engineer might miss?"
*(reveal)* **R:** "Mobileye, whose cameras help cars do exactly this, grew out of Hebrew University research. Intel bought it in 2017. Lab to product."
*(optional, any time)* **V:** "Notice the skyline: the closer we get, the more detail appears."

### 2:40–3:15 · Stop 5: *Your first product flopped. Name a smart next move.*
*(answering)* **G (Cultivating Resilience):** "This one is **Cultivating Resilience**. In this game, a wrong answer isn't the end. You retry, just like founders do."
*(reveal)* **R:** "Waze's co-founder says: fall in love with the problem, not your first solution."

### 3:15–3:50 · Stop 6: *A way to help a friend starting something new*
*(answering)* **N (Empowering Leadership):** "This is **Empowering Leadership**: sharing what you know so others succeed."
*(reveal)* **R:** "That's how Israeli founders often work. Wix's co-founder wrote that founders who were helped early pay it forward with intros and advice."
→ **N presses Space** (*Drive into Tel Aviv*).

### 3:50–4:00+ · Finale (stays on screen)
**N:** "Last stop: Tel Aviv! Congratulations to our farthest traveller…" *(read the winner)*
→ The screen switches to **Why it works** after about 9 s, or press Space.
**All four, one line each (≈ 15 s):**
- **R:** "So why does Israel's startup scene work? Connected networks where founders help founders…"
- **G:** "…a habit of learning from failure, and public money that pulled private money in…"
- **V:** "…research that becomes products, and a small home market that pushes everyone to think globally."
- **N:** "No single factor explains it. These five reinforce each other. Thanks for riding with us!"

*Buffer:* the core game takes 4:00. With finale lines and applause, plan on 4:15–4:30, inside the 3–5 minute window. If you're running long, choose the **3 min sprint** preset in the lobby.

---

**If something breaks:**
- Phones can't connect → switch tabs to `/demo` and say: "Here's a simulated class of 25 riders."
- Need a breather → press **P** to pause and **P** again to resume.
- Want to start over → press **R** and confirm. Scores clear and players stay.
